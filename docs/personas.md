# Personas

These personas come from the survey (22 students) and 3 interviews. Each one is a mix of real answers, not one real person.

## Primary persona: Nusrat, the Juggler

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 23 |
| **Study** | Final year CSE, private university |
| **Outside class** | Teaches 4 tuition students across the week, runs a club team, helps with family duties |
| **Tools today** | Memory, Canvas, Google Classroom, Gmail, Messenger groups |

**Her week**
Classes during the day. Tuition most evenings, at different times on different days. Club meetings pop up with a day's notice. Some teachers use Canvas, some use Google Classroom, some just say it in class.

**What she does now**
Keeps almost everything in her head. Starts assignments only when the due date is close. Picks what to do by marks first, then closest deadline.

**What goes wrong**
* Forgets tuition classes and club commitments, not graded work
* When a new quiz is announced, her whole week falls apart
* Tasks with no deadline sit untouched for months
* Sleep and family time take the hit when things pile up

**What she tried**
Google Calendar. She set Saturday as planning day, but something always came up on Saturday. Setting up each week took too long and the week kept changing. She quit.

**Goals**
* See her whole week in one place
* Stop missing classes she teaches
* Spend almost no time setting things up

**In her words**
> "It happened more times than I would like to admit."

> "Setting up the Google Calendar is really a problem."

## Secondary persona: Arif, the Group Chat Student

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 20 |
| **Study** | 2nd year, public university |
| **Outside class** | Tuition, two university clubs, volunteering |
| **Tools today** | Memory, WhatsApp and Messenger groups |

**His week**
Almost everything reaches him through group chats. Class info, club tasks and tuition changes all land in the same few apps, mixed in with jokes and memes.

**What he does now**
Reads a message, thinks "I'll remember that," then moves on. Uses phone notes sometimes, but never checks them again.

**What goes wrong**
* Forgets things he has already seen
* Important messages get buried under new ones
* Has forgotten deadlines more than 5 times in the last 3 months
* Rates how he manages things a 1 or 2 out of 5

**Goals**
* Save something from a chat in one step before it gets buried
* Know what's coming up without scrolling through chats

## Who CampusFlow is not for: Tanvir, the Light Load Student

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 20 |
| **Study** | 2nd year, engineering |
| **Outside class** | Mostly hobbies |

Tanvir plans one day at a time and does things as they come. He rarely forgets anything that matters. He has never tried a planner and doesn't feel he needs one.

**Why he's here:** He shows that the pain comes from a heavy load, not from being a student. CampusFlow is built for Nusrat and Arif, not Tanvir. If a feature only makes sense for Tanvir, it doesn't belong in version 1.
