
# Interview Notes

Three interviews, about 15 minutes each, September 2026. Names removed. Recordings not shared.

## P1: Undergraduate, heavy load
* Tuition all day plus studies. Part of a university club.
* Gets info from Messenger, WhatsApp and Google Classroom. The class rep posts the same thing everywhere, so she never has to search.
* Uses memory for daily tasks. Sets a phone alarm only on submission days.
* Last miss: forgot a club recruitment interview. Near miss: uploaded an assignment but forgot to press submit.
* Picks what to do by closest deadline. Struggles when there's no deadline. Academic wins over personal.
* Overload hurts her sleep and health, but she says she can manage.
* Has tried nothing to improve. Says she doesn't want it to be easier.
* Pain point: files open on her phone but not her laptop, so she moves them through Drive.
* Would change: waiting until the deadline.

## P2: Final semester CSE, heavy load
* Teaches multiple English classes, starting a business, family duties.
* Private university. Class reps exist for only some courses and don't track everything. Checks Canvas, Google Classroom and Gmail herself. Asks in section Messenger groups when she misses class.
* Keeps assignments in her head. Starts only when the due date is close.
* Often forgets the classes she teaches. Assignments don't slip because they're graded.
* Picks by marks first, then closest deadline. Tasks with no deadline stall.
* When overloaded: sleep goes first, family time disappears, grades take some hit.
* Tried a notes app and Google Calendar. Both failed. Calendar setup took too long, her week kept changing and her weekly planning day kept getting bumped.
* Says nothing in her current system works well.
* Downloads the same file many times because she can't find where it saved.
* Would change: be more disciplined, and see her whole week without a long setup.

## P3: 2nd year IEM, light load
* No job, tuition or clubs. Mostly hobbies.
* Hears about assignments from close friends by text, usually a day or two before. Doesn't rely on the class rep.
* Memory only. Says if he forgets something, it probably wasn't important.
* Plans one day at a time. Picks by priority. Sets his own deadline when there isn't one.
* When overloaded, grades go first.
* Never tried any app or planner.
* What works: doing things as they come.
* Annoyance: unplanned tasks from other people that break his day.
* Hard to find old photos on his phone.
